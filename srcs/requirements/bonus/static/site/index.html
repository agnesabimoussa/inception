AI October 16, 2026

AI assistants move from the cloud to your laptop

More assistants now run small language models directly on the device. Answers come faster, work offline, and private documents never leave the machine.

The trade-off: local models are smaller, so the hardest questions are still sent to the cloud.

A new generation of ARM laptop processors pairs efficiency cores with a dedicated AI unit. Early reviews report a full day of work on one charge, with the fans almost never spinning.

Security October 9, 2026

Browsers start blocking old TLS versions for good

Major browsers now refuse connections that only offer TLS 1.0 or 1.1. Sites must support TLS 1.2 or 1.3, the same rule this project's NGINX already follows.

Tip: check your own site with openssl s_client -connect example.com:443.
